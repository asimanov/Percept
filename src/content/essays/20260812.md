### They had us in the first half.

##### A state with zero distance gets a road built to it.

<br>

Just as I was finishing up a sketch on a Saturday evening, the tail end of the last cycle, a track called "Divine Path" by V-Society & Rave Nine came through the earbuds. A spoken sample from the mix innocently caught my attention. Its first half names the fifth dimension as a state of consciousness already within you, waiting to be awakened — no far-off destination, no distant dream. Its second half tells you to "start riding your consciousness to it."

They had us in the first half, not gonna lie. One side corrects the oldest error in this genre while the next one commits it. A state with zero distance gets a road built to it inside a single sample. Nonetheless, the first half had already done its work, and it left this thought behind:

I exist inside a three-dimensional being. Height, width, depth (x, y, z). My experience is bound to a fourth dimension, time, and is carried in one direction whether I approve or not. What if the fifth dimension is simply *within*? Is it that simple?

Dimensions are the grammar of extension. Every axis the geometers add — x, y, z, t, some hypothetical w — buys more *outside*, more room for objects to occupy and trajectories to cross. Awareness holds no coordinate on any of them, because coordinates are what appear *to* it. It would be very on-brand for reality to hide the fifth dimension there: the station that the observer occupies. You can inventory space forever and never find that station, since the inventory is taken from it.

Anyone can demonstrate this to themselves, the same way that peels the observer from the ego. Ask yourself: *am I thoughts, or do I have thoughts?* I surely have thoughts, therefore I cannot *be* the thoughts. So the one who has them resides somewhere, and the somewhere is exactly where? X? No. Y, z? No. Time? We have searched all four addresses, thoroughly, with instruments of increasing complexity. The "I am" is nowhere to be found, and yet it just wrote this sentence.

Our materialist sciences operate beautifully in three dimensions, technically four. When the math strains, physics demands more: extra dimensions curled up smaller than any probe can resolve. Hidden by scale, accepted sight unseen, seated at the table on the strength of their usefulness to an equation. Sit with that for a moment.

A dimension no one has ever observed gets full support because instruments measure hypotheticals. Meanwhile the one "hidden" position every human occupies from birth — attested directly, universally, continuously, every waking moment — never takes the center stage that it's already standing on. Too close to see, because *it is the seeing*.

The methods were never built to acknowledge it. Third-person measurement counts contents; interiority is the operator. Every measurement ever taken was taken by an experiencer, and the experiencer appears in none of the data.

Physics even confesses this, in its own dialect. A century into its most successful theory, it still cannot say from inside its own formalism what a measurement is. The description forces a distinction between the observed system and the observer; the line can be drawn almost anywhere; the observer side is presupposed and never described. The electron makes a poor trophy but a fine witness.

Grant nothing else and one fact still stands: work done in the hidden space propagates into the measured one. I do shadow work in deep meditative states (*over there*, by any stretch of the imagination), and the changes surface in my temperament, my choices, my physical days, my relationships. You do not need to go into trance to see this for yourself.

Fears faced in imagined scenes loosen their grip in lived ones. Movement rehearsed purely in the mind sharpens the measurable performance of the body. Marcus Aurelius compressed it a couple thousand years ago: the quality of your thoughts determines the quality of your life. As above, so below. Or, more correctly: as within, so without.

So here is where I stand. I cannot get behind my station of observation. What I cannot get behind, I cannot measure. What every measurement presupposes and no measurement can capture stands *behind* the system that measures. I am left with one word for it: **fundamental**. The extended dimensions emanate from this position — x, y, z, and time unfolding in front of the observer like shadows in front of a hand. Christ said as much in Thomas's record: he is the light over all things, the all that everything came from and returns to. Split a piece of wood, he is there; lift a stone, you will find him.

There is a position I keep meeting among people who have gone down the same avenues, such as projection, lucid dreaming, deep trance, DMT, etc. They come back changed and eventually with more questions than they came in with, a natural progression of events.

The experience removed their fear of death, they say, and then: *why am I rushing? What's the hurry to get there before I die? We came here for a reason, why go back while we're still here?*

My nose wrinkles every time at this general position, and for a while I suspected the wrinkle was egoic. Instead I found the offender: the language, the verbs. **Get there. Go back. Arrive. Journey.** The whole stance is constructed out of travel grammar, and the conclusion follows: if the non-physical is a destination, then death is the scheduled arrival, and early visits are cutting in line; maybe this isn't something we "should be doing." Under the aperture, the stance dissolves on contact. Nobody asks why you'd bother humming a tune if you can hear the song or describing a flower in vivid detail if you have eyes to see it.

Gene Hart calls it the other half of your life. I'd widen it: the other half of existence, of reality, of perception, of experience, of the all. And honestly, I'm not even convinced it's half. I know of the physical and I know of the non-physical, and nothing tells me that the survey is complete. Reality bonked me on the head with a fifth dimension, and I doubt it's done swinging.

Denying the importance of the other half (or its existence completely) means running at half reception for the length of a life whose stated reason, presumably, wanted the whole instrument switched on. We have forgotten, as a civilization, just how real the non-physical is, just how important it is, and how much of *here* gets settled *over there*.

Let us come back to the EDM track and the language of it all. I keep the travel verbs in my own use because I finally understand what they are for. A human who has known nothing besides roads and destinations needs a path simply to agree and show up at the threshold. That is *belief* doing its one job: the forward reach that makes experience possible. The verbs are excellent tools but poor wayfinding markers. Experience retires the road the same way it retires belief, by replacement. The track spoke to both audiences inside one sample: a road for the ones still driving, a seat for the ones who have parked.

Which is why *within* survives every round of scrutiny while staying slightly wrong. Christ closed the door on within-as-location before anyone could open it: if the kingdom were in the sky, the birds would beat you there; if it were in the sea, the fish are already home. It is within you *and* outside you. A location could never be both at once. **A position can.**

We are out of places to look for the fifth dimension except for the one space everyone has occupied since they were born. The hiding spot in plain sight. The station the observer occupies.

***Do you recognize the observer at the station? If you stand still, you'll know, or better yet remember, exactly who it is.***
