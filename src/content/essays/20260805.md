### A shadow is a reception.

##### On synchronicity, dimension, and the kingdom at zero distance.

<br>

Every step forward in the exploration of consciousness uncovers more questions, as if the process were a recursive function keeping us forever unfinished. How could it be otherwise -- the thing under study is it itself, studying itself. The question I'm after here queries reality: is this waking life, is it dreaming, is it what happens after death, is death even a good name for what's going on.

In last week's essay and field notes, I touched on the notion, like I have many times before, that "sacred numbers" and synchronicities are shadows of greater meaning, dimensions, entities. Think about your own 2D shadow as it outlines your form on the sidewalk during a sunny afternoon. You raise your hands and make a shadow bird, a dog, a rabbit. Those shadows are likenesses, made by a three-dimensional nature observing itself, arranging its phalanges in a specific order, positioning the whole body to cast a perfect shape. Intent, cast into a flatter world.

Now imagine the likeness a five-dimensional being, or greater, could cast -- on purpose or simply by existing, passing through, just being, just like you. Why couldn't it arrive as a complex data packet: a synchronicity, a number that means something to you appearing on a random afternoon in a location completely unknown to you? For all I know, the reality in which you are reading this essay is made entirely of shadows cast by higher-dimensional entities.

These shadows and our own nature, deeply intertwined in this physical waking state, are all part of one reality. Reality has always been here. The separation is manufactured by the instrument that observes it -- **you**. A shadow is a reception. The hand stays where it is. The sun stays where it is. The likeness arrives for you to interpret.

The synchronicity behaves the same way. I traveled nowhere to collect that number on that random afternoon. It came into being at the point where I already stood. The signal came to the instrument. The instrument held still and received, perceived, and interpreted.

This is where the word "higher" starts to strain. Higher-dimensional is a ladder wearing math clothes. The sidewalk stays flat through the entire performance. It climbs and weaves toward nothing and still holds every shape the hand can cast. For the receiving surface, dimension is bandwidth. The measure is capacity, aperture, and it has no altitude.

The literature of consciousness exploration runs heavy on travel grammar. Step into. Cross over. Ascend. Get out. Every verb packs a suitcase for a journey across a separation the instrument itself manufactures. The map gets drawn with distance printed on it, and lifetimes are spent crossing thresholds that were printed but never measured.

*The kingdom is within* operates at zero distance, in the present tense. The Gospel of Thomas records Christ delivering the anti-travel saying: if the kingdom were in the sky, the birds would arrive before you; if it were in the sea, the fish would already be home. It is within you and outside you. Invert it into a journey, and it stops functioning. The destination slides out to *there* and forward to *later*, and something always appears at the crossing, offering to guide you across. Because we expect it to.

***So the verbs change. I widen; what arrives, arrives on a surface that grew. I receive; the shape falls where I stand. I retune; the station changes while the radio stays on the desk.***

Awareness is the sidewalk. It goes nowhere, and everything crosses it. Every road ends where it began. The disciples asked Christ how the end would come. Have you found the beginning, he answered, that you now seek the end? The end will be where the beginning is. Blessed is the one who *stands* at the beginning. The tradition chose the stationary verb. Standing there, you know the end without traveling to meet it.

The seat that does the widening -- that watches belief reach, experience land, knowledge settle, and remembrance bring it all forward -- I have named before: agile awareness. The agility lives in the aperture, opening and closing, the only *moving* part of a stationary instrument. Asked for the evidence of the source in a person, Christ answered with two words: motion and rest.

They asked when the kingdom would come. It does not come by watching for it, Christ answered. No one will point and say *here* or *there*. It is already spread out across the earth, and people do not see it.

Turn, and the whole sky of shadows turns with you.

Expand, and what once looked like a destination will be where you are already standing.
