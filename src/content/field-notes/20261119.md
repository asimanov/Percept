### I let myself fall backward.

##### Field Notes from 20261115: An open line, a swing through the garage, and controls in the light

>Later in the same day, early evening, I popped into the kitchen to grab something before heading back out to grill our Sunday dinner's steaks. In that short time the youngest boy was unloading his Nerf gun while shouting "PEW PEW PEW" at his usual volume, which is Spinal Tap 11. That brief moment captured the essence of living with a boy who is 3 going on 4.

<br>

Decent day. I took the oldest boy to the bookstore since he's out of chapter books, the best kind of problem! Picked up some stuff for him and a couple more Dr. Seuss books for the youngest boy, because I'm tired of reading the same stuff every night, and it's a big collection to begin with.

Overall feeling good, with a decent amount of energy for a Sunday afternoon session. I lay down to do the Wave 4 NVC 2 track from The Gateway Experience. I genuinely enjoy these. Practicing NVC (non-verbal communication) in trance states is a different kind of task than imagination during waking hours.

**An aside:** The Gateway Experience is The Monroe Institute's in-home audio course for reaching and working in expanded states of awareness. It runs in albums called Waves, six exercises to a Wave, with Monroe's voice guiding the early ones.

At the end of the exercise, you move to Focus 12 (expanded awareness) and "see if you can pick up on any non-verbal communications." I've had pretty good experiences with this part, but now that I've been running through these tapes, I'm getting next to nothing. My posture is open to receive, yet nothing comes in. Instead of being upset about it, I end up practicing NVC even more, running through scenes that I think mean, "Open for contact, communication, I'm here, WHY IS NO ONE TALKING TO ME!?!?! 😭" Hasn't worked... yet.

I got impatient. I've learned that, for me, being still and awaiting is one of the hardest states to achieve, which I always need to work on. I'm making a mental note to try mini "open to NVC" sessions during the workweek to figure it out. A strong intent while in Focus 12 and settling into ***the now*** is my strategy.

After this meditation, I put my gear aside and turned over for a couple of passes at immersive altered states. The first pass went well. As soon as I lay down, I felt the pull to slip under. I didn't jump straight into projection for one reason: *while I was lying there stating my intent, **I felt my head and neck vibrate***.

It went past the low rumbling: rhythmic, "louder" than usual, and very steady. I wasn't in a hypnagogic state yet, the drowsy edge where waking gives way to sleep, and that's why it kept me awake. My curiosity was piqued. Hypnagogia is when the "rumbles" show up, not before, or so I thought.

This neck and head vibration stayed with me until I projected into Locale 1. Again, no drama, just a shift of awareness from one frequency to another. This turn I was carrying my tablet, probably because I still needed to finish Sunday's entry. I was in the living room and walking to my office, and as I started to turn toward it, I stopped myself. I looked straight ahead, where the garage door was, and then to my right, where the office is: door open, lights on, the daybed folded out of the ottoman.

**An aside:** Locales are Robert Monroe's sorting of the places he went out of body, and I add a zero to his count. Locale 0 is the physical, Locale 1 is the etheric layer built one to one off the physical, and Locale 2 is everything further along the band.

After pausing for a moment or two, I changed my mind, walked over to the garage door, opened it, and stepped in. The lights were on: the daylight LED panels I have on during the morning and daytime if I'm in there, instead of the orange LEDs hanging from the rafters for the evenings. Standing in the middle of the garage gym, workshop to my right, I paused again. Then I slowly let myself fall backward toward the floor.

My falling momentum was picked up, and I began floating backward toward the garage shop. I turned at the last minute to face the pegboards hanging on the wall. I got curiously close to them, so much so that I could see the white paint flaking off a single hole's rim and the texture of the composite underneath it.

I could look at that the entire projection, as silly as that sounds, but before I could commit, I sped away to the other side. There I flew over the squat rack and got close to the rafters, then swung back around. It felt like I was on a swing, only the swing had no forward and back, no obvious anchor, just the motion, while the direction changed from one corner of the garage gym and shop to the other.

I'm not going to lie, it was a lot of fun and carefree. Nothing of importance happened other than this swinging and floating fun. Eventually it subsided, and I entered the familiar portal travel back to the physical, where I was gently deposited on the daybed with a soft click into the body.

The second pass took a little longer to get going, but I projected briefly nonetheless. Locale 1 again, like before. Notable findings: the living room furniture was rearranged, and Moose, the German Shepherd mutt, was "fused" with the couch but seemed happy about it (he doesn't ever lie on the couch). I spent most of my time petting his beautiful face.

The Liminals upon the return to Locale 0, physical waking life, were doing more of the same from the first projection, and yet again looked like user interface controls: moving, blinking, growing, shrinking, changing and rearranging an interface that seemed to emanate from the pool of light they spawned from. It's not the first time I've seen these "controls." I'm still wondering what they are or what they may represent.

Later that evening I walked into the kitchen. The comic has the rest.
