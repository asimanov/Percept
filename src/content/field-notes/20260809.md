### The week had exactly one moving part.

##### Field Notes 20260803-20260809 - Retrospective

<br>

The week had exactly one moving part. A nasty virus set the terms -- survival mode, sessions shortened or surrendered to sleep, physical training cast aside for another week. Through all of it, the magician's table stayed fully stocked, wand, cup, sword, and pentacle each in place, and the one item missing was the very key to it all, my awareness. In its place, a box of tissues.

I dropped in to see what condition my condition was in, and the tissues aren't an accusation. Attention goes where it is needed most, consciously or unconsciously, and this week it went to the body: checking in on the team, examining the fire they're fighting, providing watchful support while processes far more capable than my thinking keep the 3D meat space suit operational. The click-outs told the same story from inside the sessions -- the ideal state arriving on time, everything humming along near automation, then a big chunk missing from the tape. You know something happened, but you can't prove it.

The mind made its own bid for the missing attention. Illness always brings the invasion, negative thinking and self-flagellation, an hour of egoic spin-out crapping all over everything in reach. What broke it was a performance I hadn't consciously staged before, the good cop, reminding the mind of the actual reality it was living through. The anchor released, the smile arrived unforced, and even the I'm-sick-shuffle got a pep in its step. You don't need to suffer in areas of your self that aren't actively suffering. Where awareness goes is voluntary. It always is.

That's the same instruction the essay carried in bigger clothes. A shadow is a reception; the hand stays where it is, the sun stays where it is, and the likeness arrives for the instrument to interpret. Nothing about this week called for travel -- not the recovery, not the recalibration, not the mood repair. The verbs changed instead: widen, receive, retune. Asked for the evidence of the source in a person, the answer came in two words, motion and rest. This week was the rest, and it counted.

So where is the seat on the agitated water? There isn't one. Stand up; maybe it wasn't that deep to begin with. The table keeps its tools, the tissues will clear themselves out, and come Monday I'm blasting energy out of my feet on a 10k to kick-start the actual return to form -- and to go pick up the key.
