### The only format that survives the crossing.

##### Field Notes of Rahgah 20260831-20260906 - Retrospective

<br>

**The third spread of Field Notes of Rahgah.**

<img src="/Media/art/sketches/20260907-S3.jpg" style="height: auto; width: 100%; border-radius: 6rem;" alt="20260907-S3">

<br>
<br>

The third spread is done. Three entries this week, each one a horizontal panel: a stone head, an open mouth, a road into the mountain. All three turned out to be about the same thing: format. What the deep material travels in, and what falls off it in transit.

The cycle opened on an admission -- I have very little interest in past lives, and what I thought was a block turned out to be the veil itself. The forgetting is chosen, and what crosses the seal doesn't arrive as story at all. What arrives is structure: disposition, orientation, the way you move before you have reasons.

Then the communication from the vessel revealed itself. Meditate hungry and the hungry footage arrives, hands moving food toward a mouth that opens on cue. The body can't talk, so it asks in pictures, and the picture is the same medium I've spent years using to send instructions the other way. While the command moves down, the petition moves up, same exact road both ways.

The week closed by naming the process. Imagination -- the word our adulthood filed under unserious while the serious world kept it running under laundered names: visualization, mental rehearsal, simulation. It gets inverted as decoration on top of the real work, but its true purpose is the protocol that the deeper layers function on, and the floor is trainable. If the screen is dark, start with plain words and let the senses follow, all of them.

The plot running through all three: **the stories don't survive these crossings; images and character do.** The signal didn't start appearing, it started being noticed. Use shadow work to retune your instrument, to widen the aperture, to reclaim what you came into this world with.

As the chapter page turned to Chapter 2, the story turned inward with it. The first act was exterior: tank, landing, path. This week's panels all point back into the vessel: memory, appetite, imagination. The serial moved inside without slowing down.

Chapter 2 is still open. Feed the vessel, use the channel, build the destination out of feeling. The arriving will handle itself.
