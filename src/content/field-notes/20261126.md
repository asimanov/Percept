### Selene and The Herm.

##### Field Notes from 20261122: Finding a leader god the old way, and what it settled about "the guides" for me

<br>

I was listening to a podcast and heard about something I'd run into before: the idea that each of us follows a "leader god," that we each have a daimon, and that apparently there's a way to figure out which. I wanted to figure it out.

**An aside:** In later Platonism, every god stands at the head of a chain, a *seira*, that runs down through spirits and souls to animals, plants and stones. A daimon is the guardian spirit tied to a soul, and Plato describes it in three ways: allotted to you, chosen by you, or the highest part of your own soul.

It turns out there are four ways to find it, and they don't agree with each other (yay). Plato's is to look at what you love and how you live. Porphyry's is the birth chart. Iamblichus told Porphyry the chart was the wrong tool and that you find out by calling on it. Plotinus said there's nobody to go looking for, because the daimon is the part of you just above the part you're living from. Why is this shit so complicated? lol

I went with the chart, because it's the one with steps... and I have it. Porphyry took his version from an older astrologer, Antiochus of Athens, and stripped down, it goes like this:

- **One. *Is it a day chart or a night chart?***
  - Mine is a night chart, so the Moon is the light that counts.
- **Two. *Which planet rules the sign that light sits in? That planet is the Master of the chart.***
  - My Moon is in Cancer, its own sign, so the Moon answers to itself.
- **Three. *Which planet rules the Moon's "term," the small slice of the sign it falls in? That one is the Co-Master.***
  - In TimePassages, it's a single line: open the Moon, open Rulerships, read Term Ruler. Mine says Mercury.
- **Four. *Trade the planets for their gods.***
  - The Moon is Selene, whom later writers tied to Artemis and to Hecate, the goddess of crossroads and the in-between. Mercury is Hermes: the messenger, the god of boundaries, and the one who guides souls across.

***Moon and Mercury, Selene and Hermes.***

A caution before I make anything of it. The old authors argue over the fine print of this method, and one of them thought the whole chart route was a mistake. I'm holding it as a map, a wayfinding tool, a local drunkard giving directions to tourists. In all seriousness, I've maintained a view of Astrology and Tarot as the starting point of inquiries which take shape based on *your interpretation that depends on your own state of being*.

Do you hold strong dogmatic fears regarding divination practices? If "yes," then you're going to see the devil and evil in all of it, no matter what is presented to you. Fear is a strong belief, and belief brings on the experience that it describes. Remember that, because this applies to your life in general.

Still, whenever new information lands like this, it's like playing a puzzle game and getting a piece right. A goddess of the in-between, for someone who named what he sees in the in-between the Liminals. Two keepers of crossings, when threshold imagery, either given or generated by me, has been a constant.

And Hermes. Earlier this year, after our dog Zoe passed, I had a projection where I hugged her and traveled through a portal with her. I wrote about it in [*Part of the Way*](https://perceptindex.com/field-notes/20260513/):

> Sitting with this a little longer, I think she came for a visit so I could help her cross over. The recognition was there, the embrace was there, and then the descent began with her -- I went part of the way and was sent back.

That is Hermes's errand, and nobody showed up to run it other than myself.

Which brings me to the guides. For a few years now, I've called on a guide in trance and come up with silence, and at some point the silence answered: *you are the guide*. That makes a lot more sense this week. In Plato, the guide of the soul and the daimon are the same office, so there is no second tier of guides on this map to answer the call. Proclus says a chain carries the name of the god at its head, and that those further down it are glad to take the same name. On that reading, I wouldn't expect to meet Hermes. I'd expect to find myself doing what he does, at my own level.

Mercury retrograde, the stretches when Mercury appears to move backward in the sky, fits here too. I could never account for why my altered states are stronger and more immersive inside that window, more of everything, and easier to initiate. That doesn't mean access is jammed outside of it. I know it isn't, and I figured that out long before any of this. What this does is the opposite of a limiting belief: it backs up a permanently thin veil at all times, with added access when Mercury turns around. *The Moon is the constant and Mercury is the variable*.

Believe it or not, this gives me a clearer picture of my situation with "the guides" than I had before, and that is a major boost to my confidence in this work. I don't receive obvious guide language. I am my own guide, not because I'm better but because of my daimon, the higher Self properties that guide my progress, as is the nature of my being.

I want to be plain that I'm not peacocking. In the tradition this comes from, every soul has a daimon. Having one is no mark of rank. The question of the guides has been a heavy one for me, something I've wanted to solve without delusions of grandeur.

So in trance I will spend time calling on them, as a direction. Calling is Iamblichus's route, the one he gave Porphyry in place of the chart, and he says where it leads: a god set over the soul as its leader, with the daimon stepping back or serving that god. I have a stronger pointer to my inquiry, a puzzle that I'm supposed to figure out.
